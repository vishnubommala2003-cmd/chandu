The Founder's Vision

"Real estate should be a wealth generator, not a source of ambiguity."

When Mr. Suman Babu Garaga established Viseshta Group in 2018, the real estate landscape in Greater Hyderabad was characterized by massive ticket sizes that excluded individual retail professionals from high-growth commercial corridors.

Under his stewardship, Viseshta Group pioneered transparent land aggregation, rigorous 30-year title due diligence, and statutory RERA compliance. Having successfully developed landmark communities including Palm Valley, Teak Village, and Vistara Valley, the group embarked on its flagship commercial venture: Invesmart.

Invesmart represents the culmination of this philosophy: allowing professionals, doctors, NRIs, and business owners to acquire Grade-A commercial IT workspace starting from Rs 30 Lakhs* with structured Day 1 rental cash flows.
