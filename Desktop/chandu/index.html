Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commercial space ownership at Invesmart.

Indicative rent is an estimated rental yield computed based on prevailing Grade-A commercial office market rates in Hyderabad's IT corridor. It reflects standard corporate lease rates per square foot but is not a fixed or guaranteed payout, remaining subject to actual tenant lease agreements signed for the premises.
Rental disbursements begin from Day 1 of the lease commencement date upon completion and formal corporate handover. For pre-leased units with corporate tenants already in place, rental yield kicks in immediately upon registration and transfer of the lease deed.
Every investor receives a complete legal bundle comprising: registered Sale Deed / Conveyance Deed, TS RERA project registration copy, sanctioned architectural floor plan, 30-year Non-Encumbrance Certificate (EC), fire NOC, and corporate tripartite lease assignment agreements where applicable.
Commercial property registration in Telangana is levied as per prevailing state government stamp duty and registration fee structures (currently approx. 7.5% stamp duty & registration plus applicable documentation charges). Our advisory team provides a precise line-item breakdown upfront.
Invesmart is planned on clear title commercial-zoned land with building sanctions, structural vetting, environmental clearances, and TS RERA compliance. [Full approval docket and RERA registration documents are available for inspection at our office or upon consultation request].
Options range from accessible modular micro-commercial spaces starting from 350 sq.ft and 750 sq.ft (from Rs 30 Lakhs*) up to half-floor (5,000 sq.ft) and full-floor corporate suites (10,000+ sq.ft) for enterprise portfolios.
